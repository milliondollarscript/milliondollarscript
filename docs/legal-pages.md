# Legal Pages

Million Dollar Script can create starter legal pages for the core plugin and participating extensions.

## Important Notice

The generated legal pages are not legal advice. Laws, platform rules, advertising requirements, privacy obligations, and refund rules vary by jurisdiction and business model. Review the generated pages with a qualified professional before relying on them.

## Recommended Pages

A pixel advertising site commonly needs:

- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- Refund and Cancellation Policy.
- Advertising Content Policy.
- ImageGrid Rendering Service Terms when hosted rendering is used.
- Extension-specific legal pages for features such as sponsor inventory, automated moderation, analytics, or hosted services.

## Review Before Publishing

Before publishing legal pages:

- Confirm the business name and site name.
- Confirm contact information or link to a contact page.
- Confirm accepted payment methods.
- Confirm refund, renewal, cancellation, and expiration rules.
- Confirm what personal data is collected from advertisers.
- Confirm how uploads, URLs, analytics events, and logs are retained.
- Confirm whether any hosted service or CDN receives creative assets.

## Updating Legal Pages

If settings, payment providers, hosted services, or extensions change, review the legal pages again. The pages should reflect the current site behavior rather than old launch assumptions.
